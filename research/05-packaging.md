# 5. Packaging: titles, thumbnails, the click

Researched 2026-11-07. Tags as in `01-monetization.md`. Titles and views from `data/channels-2026-11-07.md`;
thumbnails looked at on the channel pages in Chrome at phone width.

## The short version
- **The click is the first test, and we are failing it:** Paperback Millionaire Ep. 1 got 0.6% of 156 impressions.
- **Hit titles name something people already know or a giant number, then open a gap:** "The Wolf of Wall Street
  Scam Explained Like You're 5" (4.3M), "WTF Happened to Labubu?" (2.1M), "Who Does America Owe $40 Trillion?" (1.1M).
- **Hit thumbnails:** one recognizable face (a caricature of the real person) or a mascot, 1-4 huge words, one clear
  object. Readable at phone size.
- **The video's first 30 seconds must deliver what the title and thumbnail promised** (YouTube's own advice).

**[mine] What the hits share:**
1. **A subject the viewer already knows** (Wolf of Wall Street, Labubu, the Great Depression, Rockefeller, Anthropic)
   or **a number too big to ignore** ($40 trillion, $7.9 trillion). Known subjects get both search and browse traffic.
2. **A gap:** "explained like you're 5" (I'll finally get it), "WTF happened" (something went wrong), "who does America
   owe" (I think I know, I might be wrong), "you've never heard of" (hidden).
3. **Short and plain.** No jargon, nothing to decode.
4. **Misses are vague or far from the viewer:** "Where money comes from" (abstract), a Chinese holding company the
   viewer has no feeling about.

**Title rules for the channel:** known subject or giant number + one gap; under ~60 characters; a question or a
"what happened"; no clickbait the video doesn't pay off (01, 1.3: metadata is part of the review).

## 5.2 Thumbnails (looked at in Chrome, phone width)
- **Crayon Capital:** hand-drawn crayon caricatures of the real people (Steve Jobs beside a beige Apple computer; two
  world leaders face to face over a flag; Belfort in a crowd), 1-2 huge words naming the subject ("STEVE JOBS"),
  warm saturated background, the channel's own small mascot in the corner.
- **Fortune & Fall:** bold cartoon/3D scene, red against blue, a short claim in capitals ("ONE LAPTOP — $6.3B LOST",
  "HE WANTS YOUR RAM").
- **PENNY:** the coin-headed mascot + a short question ("IS THIS IT?", "PONZI $40 TRILLION?").
- **Ifverse:** a stick figure in silhouette, 2-3 word claim ("TOO SLOW.", "500 IQ"), dark purple night palette.
- **POV money winners** (`channels/book-stories/STRATEGY.md`): a confrontation (smug person vs calm "you") + one big
  number or document.

**Thumbnail rules:** one face or mascot with a strong expression, one object, 1-4 words that add to the title (never
repeat it), high contrast, check it at 160 px wide. A recurring mascot or drawn style = recognizable in the feed.

## 5.3 Click-through numbers
- **[report]** Widely quoted from YouTube's Help: half of channels and videos have an impressions CTR of 2-10%. Not
  confirmed on YouTube's page (the FAQ says CTR varies by content, audience and where it's shown, and not to judge it
  right after upload). New videos and new channels often fall outside that range.
- **Ours:** Ep. 1 0.6% on 156 impressions (23 h). Below the range: the packaging, not only the video, needs to change.
- **[report]** YouTube Studio's Test & Compare tests up to 3 thumbnails (and, per vidIQ, titles); the winner is
  reportedly picked by watch time per impression, not by clicks. Use it on every video.

## 5.4 Matching the click to the video
**[YouTube]** Studio's retention report shows how many are still watching at 30 seconds. YouTube's own advice when that
is low: change the first 30 seconds, or change the title and thumbnail to match the video better.
**[mine]** Write the title and thumbnail first, then make the cold open (04, 4.3) show the thumbnail's thing within
5 seconds and state the title's gap within 30.

## 5.5 Decisions this part asks for
1. Title and thumbnail are written before the script, and the topic is dropped if no strong pair exists.
2. One drawn style and one mascot for the channel's thumbnails.
3. Test & Compare on every video; read CTR at 24 h and 72 h, not before.
