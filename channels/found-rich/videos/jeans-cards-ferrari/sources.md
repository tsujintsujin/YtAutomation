# Sources

- Levi's jeans from the 1880s, found by a "denim archaeologist" in an abandoned mine in the American West; only a couple of similar pairs are known (in museums); sold October 2022 at the Durango Vintage Festivus near Aztec, New Mexico, for $87,400 including the buyer's premium: [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Odd_News/2022/10/13/Levis-jeans-from-1880s-auctioned-for-87400-after-mine-shaft-discovery/7851665692149/), [CNN via ABC13](https://abc13.com/post/levis-jeans-auction-new-mexico/12328943/)
- Black Swamp Find: about 700 E98 baseball cards (1910, given out with candy) found in February 2012 in a box in the attic of grandfather Carl Hench's house in Defiance, Ohio, by cousins Karl Kissner and Karla Hench; the family believes he gave cards out at his meat market; nearly pristine; 37 of them sold by Heritage Auctions in Baltimore on 2 August 2012 for $566,132: [Fox News / AP](https://www.foxnews.com/us/rare-baseball-cards-unearthed-in-ohio-home-sell-for-more-than-500g-at-auction), [Sports Collectors Digest](https://sportscollectorsdigest.com/cards/black-swamp-find-makes-debut-with-566132-in-sales)
- Baillon collection: about 60 cars left in barns and sheds by French collector Roger Baillon, whose museum plan failed for lack of money; his heirs opened the barns in 2014 after about 50 years; a 1961 Ferrari 250 GT SWB California Spider, once owned by actor Alain Delon, protected under stacks of magazines; sold by Artcurial at Retromobile in Paris on 6 February 2015 for €16.3 million including premium: [Old Cars Weekly](https://www.oldcarsweekly.com/news/baillon-collection-world-record-auction-price-ferrari), [Italpassion](https://www.italpassion.fr/en/ferrari/the-story-of-a-mythical-ferrari-found-in-a-french-barn-and-finally-sold-50-years-later-for-a-record-price/)
