# Sources

- 1865 Brooklyn Atlantics card: a Maine man (anonymous) bought an old photo album, old soda bottles and a couple of oak chairs for under $100 at a yard sale in Baileyville, Maine; in the album was an original 1865 photo of the Brooklyn Atlantics mounted on card; the Library of Congress holds a card of the team it calls the first dated baseball card (reports differ on whether the two are copies of one negative); sold at Saco River Auction Co., Biddeford, Maine, on 6 February 2013 for $92,000 including premium: [Christian Science Monitor / AP](https://www.csmonitor.com/Business/Latest-News-Wires/2013/0207/Rare-1865-baseball-card-fetches-92K-at-Maine-auction), [MLB](https://mlb.com/cut4/update-the-oldest-known-baseball-card-found-at-a-yard-sale-has-sold-for-92000/c-40902430)
- 1943 copper penny: Don Lutes Jr. of Pittsfield, Massachusetts, got it in his school cafeteria change in 1947 at 16; 1943 pennies were zinc-coated steel to save copper for the war, and a few copper ones were struck by mistake; the Treasury told him none were made; he kept it all his life (he died in 2018); sold at Heritage Auctions in January 2019 for $204,000, proceeds to the Pittsfield public library (Berkshire Athenaeum): [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/rare-1943-penny-sells-for-200000-far-below-expectations-heritage-auctions-don-lutes-jr/), [Artnet](https://news.artnet.com/market/rare-1943-penny-auction-1436167)
- The "tenner" diamond: a woman bought a box of costume jewellery for £10 at a car boot sale in Isleworth, West London, in the late 1980s; she wore the ring for years thinking it was fake; about thirty years later a local jeweller suspected a real diamond; a 26.27-carat cushion-cut diamond; sold at Sotheby's London in June 2017 for £656,750: [Sotheby's](https://www.sothebys.com/en/articles/a-rare-coveted-diamond-discovered-at-a-car-boot-sale?locale=en), [ITV](https://itv.com/news/2017-06-07/car-boot-sale-ring-10-sells-for-656-000-at-auction), [Marie Claire](https://marieclaire.co.uk/news/fashion-news/10-diamond-ring-513977)
