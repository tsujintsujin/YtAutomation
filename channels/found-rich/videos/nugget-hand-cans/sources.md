# Sources

- Ballarat nugget: an anonymous amateur prospector with a hand-held metal detector found a 5.5 kg gold nugget about 60 cm underground near Ballarat, Victoria, in January 2013, in an area searched since the 1850s gold rush; valued at more than A$300,000 by Cordell Kent of the Ballarat Mining Exchange gold shop: [Live Science](https://livescience.com/26371-gold-nugget-ballarat-australia.html), [Perth Mint](https://www.perthmint.com/news/collector/coin-collecting/giant-gold-nugget-discovered-in-australia/), [PRI](https://www.pri.org/stories/2013-01-17/australian-amateur-prospector-digs-massive-gold-nugget-near-ballarat-video)
- Hand of Faith: found by Kevin Hillier with a metal detector near Kingower, Victoria, on 26 September 1980, 30 cm below the surface, standing upright; 27.21 kg (875 troy ounces), the largest gold nugget found with a metal detector; sold through Kovac's to the Golden Nugget casino for over US$1 million; on show at the Golden Nugget Las Vegas: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hand_of_Faith), [Bendigo Advertiser](https://www.bendigoadvertiser.com.au/story/5704525/hand-of-faith-replica-given-to-central-deborah-gold-mine/)
- Saddle Ridge Hoard: an anonymous couple walking their dog on their Northern California property in February 2013 saw a rusty can poking out of the ground, pried it loose with a piece of wood, then found eight cans in all with 1,427 gold coins dated 1847-1894, many uncirculated; who buried them is unknown; estimated at about $10 million: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddle_Ridge_Hoard), [Popular Science](https://www.popsci.com/science/saddle-ridge-hoard-history/), [Coin World](https://www.coinworld.com/news/california-couple-finds-large-gold-coin-hoard.html)
- Taken from: "10 Lucky Finds That Made People Rich" (Top Lists: the Ballarat nugget) and "Luckiest Discoveries That Made People Rich" (Top Trending: the Hand of Faith). The Saddle Ridge cans are our own find (checked above). Facts only; every line is new wording.
