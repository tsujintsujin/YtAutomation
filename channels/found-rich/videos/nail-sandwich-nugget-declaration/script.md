Which of these would you have thrown in the trash? A stage worker in Dublin was cleaning up after a Lady Gaga show. On the floor he spotted something small and shiny. He thought it was a guitar pick. It was one of her fake nails. He sold it online for about twelve thousand dollars.

A woman in Miami did even better with her lunch. She made a grilled cheese sandwich, took one bite, and saw a face in the toast. To her, it looked like the Virgin Mary. So she kept it. For ten years. In 2004, an online casino paid twenty-eight thousand dollars for it.

Then came a single chicken nugget. In 2021, someone got a McDonald's nugget shaped like a crewmate from the game Among Us. They put it on eBay for ninety-nine cents. A hundred and eighty-four bids later, it sold for ninety-nine thousand, nine hundred and ninety-seven dollars.

But the biggest find cost four dollars. A man bought an old painting at a flea market in Pennsylvania. He only wanted the frame. When he took it apart, he found a folded paper hidden behind the canvas. It was an original copy of the Declaration of Independence, printed in 1776. Only about two dozen are known. In 1991 it sold for two point four million dollars. Nine years later it sold again, for eight million.

So next time you clean out the attic, look twice.
