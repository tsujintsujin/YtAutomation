# A painting above a stove sold for €24 million

An old woman kept a small painting in her kitchen, right above the stove. It sold for twenty-four million euros. But first, two other kitchen things that were worth a fortune.

In twenty twenty, a man in Connecticut stopped at a yard sale. He paid thirty-five dollars for a small white bowl with blue flowers. He had a feeling it was special, so he sent photos to Sotheby's. It was made in China about six hundred years ago, in the Ming dynasty. Only six others like it are known. In twenty twenty-one, it sold for over seven hundred twenty thousand dollars.

Then a man in England bought a small broken teapot for fifteen pounds. It turned out to be one of the first pieces of porcelain, that's fine china, ever made in America, from the seventeen sixties. It sold for five hundred seventy-five thousand pounds, and a museum in New York ended up with it.

Now, the painting. In twenty nineteen, an expert was clearing out an old woman's house in France. In the kitchen, above the hot plate, hung a tiny painting of Jesus. The woman thought it was just an old religious picture. It was painted around twelve eighty by Cimabue, one of the first great Italian painters. It sold for twenty-four million euros.

## Why it is built this way

- One theme: kitchen things (a bowl, a teapot, a painting above a stove).
- Hook teases the biggest find (the painting); smallest to biggest: $720,000, £575,000 (about $800,000), €24 million.
- "Porcelain" is said in plain words right after it ("that's fine china").
- The painting shows Jesus, so no picture may forbid a religious figure (SHORTS_RULES: a NEVER must not fight the line).
