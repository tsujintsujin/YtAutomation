# Sources

- Cheetozard: Paul Bartlett, owner of 1st & Goal Collectibles in Canton, Georgia, bought a Flamin' Hot Cheeto shaped like Charizard on eBay for $350 around 2019; it sat forgotten in the store's safe for about five years; he then sold it to Arena Club for $10,000; it sold at Goldin in March 2025 for $87,840 including the buyer's premium: [Artnet](https://news.artnet.com/art-world/cheetozard-flamin-hot-cheeto-auction-2616994), [Today](https://www.today.com/today/amp/rcna194921), [Hyperallergic](https://hyperallergic.com/cheeto-shaped-like-pokemon-sells-for-87k-at-auction)
- Nintendo PlayStation: Terry Diebold bought a box of belongings at the auction of the bankrupt company Advanta for $75; years later his son found the console in the family attic; a prototype from the failed early-1990s Nintendo and Sony partnership, about 200 reportedly made, the only one known to survive; sold at Heritage Auctions on 6 March 2020 for $360,000: [Just Collecting](https://news.justcollecting.com/nintendo-playstation-prototype-video-game-console-auction-sold-heritage/), [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/tech-news/featured-tech-news/joao-silva/nintendo-playstation-console-sold-at-auction-for-300k/)
- Apollo 11 lunar sample bag: Nancy Lee Carlson, a lawyer from the Chicago suburbs, bought it in 2015 for $995 at a U.S. Marshals government auction; she sent it to NASA for testing, which found Apollo 11 moon dust; it held the first lunar samples picked up on the Moon (1969); NASA kept it, she won in court (December 2016) and got it back in February 2017; sold at Sotheby's on 20 July 2017 for $1,812,500: [collectSPACE](https://www.collectspace.com/news/news-052017c-sothebys-apollo11-moon-rock-bag-auction.html), [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/apollo-11-moon-dust-samples-go-up-for-auction-against-nasas-wishes-180979859/)
