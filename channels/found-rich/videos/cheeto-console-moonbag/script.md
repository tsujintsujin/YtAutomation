# A $995 bag held dust from the moon

A woman paid nine hundred ninety-five dollars for an old zipper bag. It had dust from the moon inside, and it sold for one point eight million. But first, two more strange buys that turned into big money.

In Georgia, a man who owns a card shop bought a single Cheeto online for three hundred fifty dollars. It was shaped just like the Pokémon Charizard. Then he forgot about it. It sat in his safe for about five years. When he found it again, he sold it for ten thousand dollars. And in twenty twenty-five, it sold again at an auction, for over eighty-seven thousand dollars. For one Cheeto.

Then a man bought a box of old stuff at an auction for seventy-five dollars. The box came from the office of a company that went broke. It sat in his attic for years. Then his son looked inside and found a strange game console. Nintendo and Sony had made it together, back when they were partners. Only about two hundred were made, and it's the only one known to still exist. In twenty twenty, it sold for three hundred sixty thousand dollars.

Now, the bag. In twenty fifteen, a lawyer near Chicago saw it on a government auction website. She paid nine hundred ninety-five dollars. She sent it to NASA to be tested. There was moon dust inside. It was the bag from the very first moon landing, in nineteen sixty-nine. It held the first bits of the moon anyone ever picked up. NASA wanted to keep it, so she went to court, and she won. In twenty seventeen, the bag sold for one point eight million dollars.

What strange thing would you buy?

## Why it is built this way

- One theme: strange, cheap buys (a single Cheeto, a box of office junk, a zipper bag).
- Hook teases the biggest find (the bag); smallest to biggest: $87,840, $360,000, $1.8 million.
- The Cheeto: the shop owner got $10,000; the $87,840 was the next sale. The script says both, so nobody thinks he got $87,840.
- No real people are named; the astronaut who used the bag is not named or drawn as anyone real.
- Charizard is named in the voice only; the pictures show a curly orange-less snack shaped like a little winged dragon, never the Pokémon itself.
