# Book stories (working name): the new long-video niche

Decided by Justin 2026-10-30. Why: LuckySticks' views come almost only from the Philippines; the target is the
US. Model channel: POV Finance (@povfinanceus, ~28K subs, ~30-min illustrated narrated videos, money topics,
older US audience).

## The format
- Long videos, **15-20 minutes** each, that carry the ideas of a money book, starting with *Rich Dad Poor Dad*
  (`References/Rich-Dad-Poor-Dad-eBook.pdf`).
- **Storytelling, third person, our own storyline.** Never the book's words, names or places; the book's
  general thought must still come through the narration.
- A **character sheet** per book keeps every character the same in every picture: `rich-dad/CHARACTERS.md`.
- **Image prompts are standalone every time** (Gemini remembers nothing): each prompt carries the full prompt
  block of every character in it, the place and the style, written out in full, in story-scene order.
- New channel set to the US from day one (country United States, English (US) on every upload).

## Rich Dad Poor Dad plan
About 15-18 videos for the whole book (more if short chapters get their own story arc). Chapter One = 3 videos:
1. **The lead quarters**: the boys' fake-coin scheme, the teacher father sends them to Frank, "take it or leave
   it", dusting shelves for 10 cents an hour, ready to quit.
2. **You're the problem**: the confrontation, "life pushes you around", the poor and middle class work for
   money, the rich have money work for them; fear keeps people in jobs; now working for nothing.
3. **The rat race**: the raise offers up to $5 an hour refused, fear and greed, the donkey and the carrot, a job
   is a short-term fix for a long-term problem; the comic-book library makes money while they're away.
