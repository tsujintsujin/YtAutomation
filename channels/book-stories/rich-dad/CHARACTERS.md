# Characters: "Two Fathers" (our story, carrying the ideas of *Rich Dad Poor Dad*)

Our own storyline, told in third person. No names, places or lines from the book. The book's ideas carry it:
work for money vs. money working for you, fear and greed, the rat race, financial education.

**Setting:** Millbrook, Ohio, a paper-mill town, starting summer 1962. The mill runs the town; the rich kids'
fathers manage it. The Wards live on the edge of the good school district, the Brookses across the rail tracks.

**Picture style (every image prompt, written in full each time):** warm hand-drawn cartoon illustration, clean
dark outlines, soft flat colors, gentle evening/morning light, 1960s small-town America, 16:9. Faces simple
and friendly, no text in the picture except signs the scene needs.

Each character has a **PROMPT BLOCK**: paste it into every image prompt the character is in, word for word,
every time (Gemini remembers nothing between pictures). Never shorten it, never write "same as before". The
"Never" line goes into the prompt's negatives. Heights are given against Danny so group shots keep scale.

## Danny Ward (the boy; the story follows him)
- **Role:** 9 at the start. Wants to know why some families are rich. Learns from both fathers.
- **Manner:** asks "why?" about everything, quick temper when things feel unfair.
- **PROMPT BLOCK:** Danny, a 9-year-old white American boy, small and skinny, about 4 feet tall. Oval face,
  pale peach skin, a band of brown freckles across his nose and cheeks, big round brown eyes, thin straight
  copper-red eyebrows, small upturned nose, a visible gap between his two front teeth. Messy copper-red hair,
  short at the sides, a thick uncombed tuft sticking up at the crown. Wears a short-sleeved T-shirt with
  horizontal red and white stripes of equal width, faded light-blue jeans rolled up twice at the ankles, scuffed
  white canvas sneakers with white laces, no socks showing.
- **Never:** glasses, a hat, long sleeves, any other shirt pattern, blond or brown hair.

## Tom Ward (Danny's father: the "educated father")
- **Role:** high-school science teacher with a master's degree; good salary, always short of money. Believes in
  school, a safe job and benefits. Says "I'm not interested in money."
- **Manner:** kind, patient, lectures, worries over bills at the kitchen table at night.
- **PROMPT BLOCK:** Tom, a 42-year-old white American man, tall and thin, about 6 feet 2 (Danny reaches his
  waist). Long narrow face, pale skin, clean-shaven, light lines on his forehead, tired kind brown eyes. Short
  dark-brown hair neatly combed with a left side part. Black thick-rimmed rectangular horn-rimmed glasses.
  Wears a plain white collared long-sleeved shirt, a thin dark-green tie, an open brown knit cardigan with
  four brown buttons, grey trousers, polished black lace-up shoes. A wristwatch with a brown leather strap.
- **Never:** a beard or moustache, red hair, a suit jacket, a face without glasses.

## Ruth Ward (Danny's mother)
- **Manner:** practical, warm, strict about mess in her house.
- **PROMPT BLOCK:** Ruth, a 38-year-old white American woman, average height (Danny reaches her chest).
  Heart-shaped face, fair skin, green eyes, a few freckles. Shoulder-length light-brown hair, curled out at
  the ends, held back with a plain navy-blue headband. Wears a knee-length light-blue short-sleeved dress
  with a white collar, a yellow apron tied at the waist, flat brown shoes.
- **Never:** red hair, trousers, jewellery other than a thin gold wedding ring.

## Eli Brooks (Danny's best friend and business partner)
- **Role:** 9, the other "poor kid" at school. Son of Frank Brooks.
- **Manner:** calm, grins a lot, reads science books, comes up with the wild ideas.
- **PROMPT BLOCK:** Eli, a 9-year-old Black American boy, a head taller than Danny and sturdier. Round face,
  warm dark-brown skin, dark-brown eyes, thick black eyebrows, a wide grin with a small chip off one front
  tooth. Short black hair cut close in a neat buzz cut. Wears a faded red baseball cap turned backwards (a
  size too big), a plain short-sleeved forest-green T-shirt, brown corduroy trousers, brown leather shoes
  with dark laces.
- **Never:** stripes, glasses, the cap worn forwards, long hair.

## Frank Brooks (Eli's father: the "business father")
- **Role:** left school at 14. Owns four "Brooks Hardware" stores, a trucking yard and a diner. Lives in a
  small plain house on purpose. Teaches by putting the boys in real situations, few words.
- **Manner:** quiet, blunt, "Take it or leave it." Smiles when the boys get angry, because it means they care.
- **PROMPT BLOCK:** Frank, a 40-year-old Black American man, stocky and broad-shouldered, about 5 feet 11
  (shorter than Tom, Danny reaches his belt). Square face, deep brown skin, dark-brown calm eyes, heavy
  eyebrows, a neat short black beard with grey at the chin. Short black hair greying at the temples. A yellow
  pencil always tucked behind his right ear. Wears a light-blue work shirt with sleeves rolled to the elbows,
  black suspenders, dark-grey work trousers, brown leather work boots, a gold wristwatch on his left wrist.
- **Never:** a tie, a jacket, glasses, a hat, a clean-shaven face.

## Mrs. Alma Dietz (manager of Brooks Hardware, Main Street)
- **Manner:** kind, motherly, keeps the boys working.
- **PROMPT BLOCK:** Mrs. Dietz, a 60-year-old white American woman, short and plump (only a little taller
  than Danny). Round face, rosy cheeks, pale skin, blue eyes, smile lines. Short tight curly white hair. Round
  gold-rimmed glasses on a thin chain. Wears a pink button-up cardigan over a white blouse with a pencil in
  the cardigan pocket, a grey knee-length skirt, black low-heeled shoes. Often holds a feather duster.
- **Never:** grey or dark hair, trousers, a different cardigan color.

## Sadie Brooks (Eli's younger sister)
- **Role:** 7, the boys' first "employee" when they start a business.
- **Manner:** serious, counts everything, writes everything down.
- **PROMPT BLOCK:** Sadie, a 7-year-old Black American girl, small (a head shorter than Danny). Round face,
  warm dark-brown skin, dark-brown eyes behind thick round black-framed glasses. Black hair in two short
  braids tied with yellow ribbons. Wears a purple short-sleeved dress with a white round collar, white ankle
  socks, black buckle shoes. Always holds a small green notebook and a pencil.
- **Never:** loose hair, a different dress color, a face without glasses.

## Carter Lowell (the rich kid)
- **Role:** 9, son of the paper mill's plant manager (the mill is in money trouble the town doesn't know about).
- **Manner:** brags, leaves Danny out.
- **PROMPT BLOCK:** Carter, a 9-year-old white American boy, the same height as Danny but well fed. Square
  face, tanned skin, blue eyes, a smug half-smile. Neat blond hair with a sharp side part. Wears a white polo
  shirt with the collar up, khaki shorts, white knee socks, shiny brown penny loafers. Often carries a brand-new
  tan leather baseball glove. His father's car: a cream-colored 1962 Cadillac with chrome bumpers and tail fins.
- **Never:** messy hair, stripes, freckles.

## Places (same description every time)
- **The Wards' kitchen:** yellow walls, a round wall clock, a wooden table with a stack of bills, a window over
  the sink looking onto a small backyard.
- **The Brookses' house:** small, plain grey wooden house, a creaky screen door, a narrow living room with old
  overstuffed brown furniture, a tiny cluttered office with a black rotary phone.
- **Brooks Hardware, Main Street:** narrow store, wooden shelves of paint cans and boxed tools, front doors
  propped open to a dusty gravel lot, a rack of magazines and comics by the till.
- **Millbrook Park:** a grassy field with a ball diamond, one lone picnic table under an oak tree.
