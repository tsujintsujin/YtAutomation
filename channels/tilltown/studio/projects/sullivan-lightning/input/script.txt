One man was struck by lightning seven times. And he lived through every single one. Roy Sullivan was a park ranger in Virginia. In nineteen forty-two, lightning hit him in a fire lookout tower. It burned a hole right through his shoe. Twenty-seven years later, it found him again. In his truck, this time. It burned off his eyebrows. Then it got his shoulder. Then his hair caught fire. Twice. Then his ankle. The seventh time, he was out fishing. The bolt hit him right on top of the head. And then a bear tried to steal his fish. So he hit it with a stick. Lucky or cursed?
