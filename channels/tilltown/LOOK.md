# Tilltown: the look, from the references

Written 2026-11-07 from real frames (Chrome, video player, not thumbnails). **Every rule names the frame it comes from.**
No visual is built from anything else. The reference frames are kept for study only (not in git: other channels' work).

## The rules
| # | Rule | From |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every shape has a black outline, ~6-8 px at 1080p | [C] all frames; [P] village, stable |
| 2 | Flat fills, plus one soft shade on the side away from the light | [C] four men (grey on one side of heads and bodies); [P] man with globe |
| 3 | A soft blurred grey shadow on the ground under every character and prop | [C] beach chair, four men; [P] man, money bags |
| 4 | Characters: big round heads (about a third of their height), dot eyes, brows that carry the feeling, a small mouth; stick limbs for crowds, real clothes for named people | [C] Jordan (hair, shirt), four men; [P] coin mascot (glasses, vest), globe man |
| 5 | Every scene is a whole drawn place: never a plain backdrop | [C] pier, beach, warehouse, office; [P] stable, village |
| 6 | Money is shown as things: green bills, money bags, coin stacks | [C] beach, four men; [P] stable, globe man |
| 7 | Words on screen are hand-lettered capitals, in white boxes with a black outline when they label something | [C] map labels; [P] text card |
| 8 | Light sets the mood: a flat sky with outlined clouds by day; a spotlight cone or a warm glow at night | [C] beach, warehouse; [P] village, stable |
| 9 | Things move: characters act, numbers count, charts draw, the camera moves | [K] 4:32-7:05 |
| 10 | Tilltown is our own tiny world (the place where mechanisms are acted out), drawn under rules 1-8 | [P] village (the device), not its houses |
